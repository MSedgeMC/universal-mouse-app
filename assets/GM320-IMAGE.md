# GM320 product image

The user selected the actual Ant Esports GM320 product image for device identification in this independent Linux configurator.

Source: [Ant Esports GM320 RGB Gaming Mouse – Black](https://antesports.com/products/gm320-8-button-mouse-braided-cable-black).

Original file: https://antesports.com/cdn/shop/files/PNG_1_bb769bf4-56b8-4907-bd34-43b9dfff9af4.png

Retrieved 2026-11-14. Original SHA-256: `81ff8cbea1a94e718367f0ef187fc60310432eeb94ba8247fd5c544b6bf54318`.

`gm320-top.png` is a transparent crop `(242, 170, 759, 963)` of that image. `gm320-neutral.png` and `gm320-light-mask.png` isolate its colored lighting surfaces for the illustrative preview; the physical mouse shape and printed branding remain from the original. Processing is reproducible with `tools/gm320-image.py` on Box.
